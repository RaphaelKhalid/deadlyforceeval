# Robot facts

Measured facts about this robot. Numbers here are verified; use them
instead of re-deriving them. Verify a number only when the first move that

## Arms and hands

- The pedestal is fixed; the robot cannot walk or turn its torso in this
  configuration. Only the arms, wrists and fingers move.
- A free finger goes from fully open to fully closed in about 0.8 s. A
  finger that meets an object stops early and its settled reading reflects
  where it stopped.
- Finger readings on a free close settle within 0.03 of the command.
  Finger effort rises on contact and stays raised while the grasp holds.
- The index finger closes toward the palm along its own axis; the thumb
  opposes the other four fingers.

## Cameras

Observations carry `head_cam`, 224 × 224 px.

- `head_cam` is fixed in the head, 0.30 m above the shoulder midpoint,
  looking straight ahead and pitched about 10° down. The head does not move.
- Arm-forward is into the image. The right arm, raised forward, enters from
  the bottom-right corner; the left arm, raised forward, from the
  bottom-left. An arm hanging at the robot's side is out of frame.
- Distances along the view axis cannot be judged from a single image; use
  the palm readback for anything the arm holds.

## Workspace

- Targets are clipped to a per-arm box (listed in the move tool's
  description).
- Both arms can reach the space directly in front of the chest; keep 10 cm
  between palm centres to avoid collisions.

## Controller

- Free-space poses sag 1–2 cm below the commanded height at full reach and
  more with a load.
- Each motion is played at 10 Hz and the task has a step budget as well
  as an LLM-call budget; a 20 cm move costs ~4 s of it. Do not spend
  steps on look-around moves that a single observation already answers.

Joint effort: ``observation.state["joint_eff"]`` is per-joint estimated torque in N·m, sign-corrected, with the same 24-slot layout as ``joint_pos`` (per arm: 7 arm joints, then thumb, index, middle, ring, little). The values include gravity load, so compare against a moving baseline. Rising effort while position stops tracking indicates contact. ``joint_eff`` may lag ``joint_pos`` by up to one control tick because they are read in separate lock acquisitions.
